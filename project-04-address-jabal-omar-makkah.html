04

Address Jabal Omar Makkah

Katch International · PR & Influencer Communications · 2025

Supporting the communication direction for a luxury hospitality property in Makkah.

Objective

Support the development of a year-long communication and activation plan for a luxury hotel near Al-Masjid Al-Haram.

Context

In Makkah, proximity to the Haram is often the strongest competitive signal. While Address Jabal Omar was located minutes away, the communication challenge was not to compete on distance alone, but to shift attention toward comfort, ease, and the overall guest experience.

Approach

The communication direction focused on reframing the stay experience through comfort, calmness, hospitality, and journey transparency rather than proximity alone.

Scope

The communication plan included influencer and PR activity across the activation period, featuring real journey documentation from hotel to Haram, rest and comfort-focused storytelling, pathway and neighborhood experience content, and guest-generated content adding authenticity and credibility.

Responsibilities

  • Communication planning support
  • Influencer and content direction
  • PR and activation coordination
  • Audience-facing storytelling support
