Cucina Riyadh

Katch International · Restaurant Launch Communications · 2025

A restaurant launch shaped through preview dining, media relations, and a wider culinary platform.

Objective

Launch Cucina Riyadh with a clear communication direction that introduces the dining concept to media, influencers, and the wider restaurant audience.

Context

The project required building visibility around a new Marriott dining concept while coordinating launch messaging, preview moments, and post-launch coverage.

Approach

The communication stream focused on Cucina’s dining experience, visual atmosphere, and media-facing story ahead of public opening.

Scope

Pre-Launch

A private media dinner was organized ahead of launch, including curated guest lists, media and influencer coordination, tailored media briefs, and on-ground coordination with hotel and culinary teams.

Launch

Managed the transition from preview to public opening while monitoring coverage and maintaining a clear Cucina narrative across launch communication.

Post-Launch — Dinner Incredible Riyadh

Cucina later participated in Dinner Incredible Riyadh, an international culinary platform bringing together renowned chefs under the theme Saudi Meets the World. Media and influencer outreach was coordinated around the event’s international positioning.

Results

Extended visibility beyond launch phase and positioned Cucina within a broader international culinary conversation.

Press coverage included Arab News and Destination KSA.
