02

Medina Destination Strategy

Research · 2024

Benchmark research supporting the communication direction for Medina as a destination brand.

Objective

Conduct structured benchmark analysis to support the strategic development of Medina’s destination communication.

Context

Medina’s communication was largely centered around pilgrimage and spiritual obligation. While accurate, the framing limited the destination to a single narrative despite its broader cultural and historical depth.

Research

Two benchmark references shaped the analysis:

Rome

A city balancing religious significance with cultural, historical, and experiential identity without forcing a trade-off between them.

AlUla

A destination structured through multiple storytelling platforms including heritage, arts, festivals, and culture.

Finding

The limitation was not in the message itself, but in the communication architecture. Medina was being expressed through one dominant narrative while the destination naturally existed across multiple dimensions.

Responsibilities

  • Competitive and benchmark analysis
  • Desk Research
  • Contribution to internal briefs
