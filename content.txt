**PROJECT 02 — Medina Destination Strategy**

Extend The Ad Network · Strategy Research · 2024

Benchmark research supporting the communication direction for Medina as a destination brand.

OBJECTIVE
Conduct structured benchmark analysis to support the strategic development of Medina's destination communication.

CONTEXT
Medina's communication was largely centered around pilgrimage and spiritual obligation. While accurate, the framing limited the destination to a single narrative despite its broader cultural and historical depth.

RESEARCH
Two benchmark references shaped the analysis:

Rome — A city balancing religious significance with cultural, historical, and experiential identity without forcing a trade-off between them.

AlUla — A destination structured through multiple storytelling platforms including heritage, arts, festivals, and culture.

FINDING
The limitation was not in the message itself, but in the communication architecture. Medina was being expressed through one dominant narrative while the destination naturally existed across multiple dimensions.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Competitive and benchmark analysis · Research framework development · Findings synthesis · Contribution to internal strategic briefs

**PROJECT 03 — Baby Expo Riyadh**

Katch International · Launch Communications · 2025

A market-entry campaign built around relevance, precision, and adaptability.

OBJECTIVE
Launch Baby Expo in Saudi Arabia and drive awareness and attendance for its Riyadh debut.

CONTEXT
The campaign operated under two major constraints: a limited budget in a market with high media and influencer costs, and a one-month permit delay that compressed the launch timeline.

APPROACH
The campaign shifted from a paid-media-heavy model toward PR and influencer-led execution. Creator selection focused on audience alignment rather than follower count, targeting family and lifestyle creators, expecting parents, and educational parenting voices.

DURING THE DELAY
While permits were pending, educational creators produced non-promotional parenting content focused on childcare and newborn preparation, helping maintain audience interest before the official launch campaign began.

SCOPE
Influencer outreach and coordination · Content toolkit development · Messaging and talking points · Media outreach and press support · Coverage reporting · Speaker coordination support

RESULTS
22M+ organic reach · 31 on-ground influencers · 43 social media collaborations · 21 media features

Press coverage included Time Out Riyadh and What's On Saudi Arabia.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Client communication management · Influencer coordination · Media planning support · Content toolkit development · Timeline and deliverable management

**PROJECT 04 — Address Jabal Omar Makkah**

Katch International · PR & Influencer Communications · 2025

Supporting the communication direction for a luxury hospitality property in Makkah.

OBJECTIVE
Support the development of a year-long communication and activation plan for a luxury hotel near Al-Masjid Al-Haram.

CONTEXT
In Makkah, proximity to the Haram is often the strongest competitive signal. While Address Jabal Omar was located minutes away, the communication challenge was not to compete on distance alone, but to shift attention toward comfort, ease, and the overall guest experience.

APPROACH
The communication direction focused on reframing the stay experience through comfort, calmness, hospitality, and journey transparency rather than proximity alone.

SCOPE
The communication plan included influencer and PR activity across the activation period, featuring real journey documentation from hotel to Haram, rest and comfort-focused storytelling, pathway and neighborhood experience content, and guest-generated content adding authenticity and credibility.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Communication planning support · Influencer and content direction · PR and activation coordination · Audience-facing storytelling support
